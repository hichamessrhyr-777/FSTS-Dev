FSTS — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

FSTS respects the privacy of its users and is committed to handling information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, processed, and used when interacting with the FSTS Discord Bot.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, FSTS may process the following information:

* Discord User IDs.
* Discord Server IDs.
* Channel and Role IDs.
* Server configuration and bot settings.
* Command interactions and relevant operational logs.

We do not intentionally collect passwords or Discord account login credentials.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

* Provide and maintain bot functionality.
* Save server configurations and preferences.
* Troubleshoot technical issues.
* Improve reliability and security.
* Respond to support requests.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users’ personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate and maintain the bot.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable obligations.

Users or server administrators may contact the FSTS support team to request information about data deletion.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

FSTS operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord’s own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the FSTS support team through our official Discord server.
