Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

FSTS respects user privacy and aims to handle information responsibly.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, FSTS may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, server settings, and command interactions.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide bot features, maintain server configurations, troubleshoot issues, and improve reliability.

4. Data Sharing

FSTS does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the service.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained according to operational needs and applicable obligations. Users may contact the FSTS team to ask about their data and deletion requests.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

FSTS operates through Discord. Discord's own privacy policy also applies to your use of its platform.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when necessary. Changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the FSTS support team through its official Discord server.
